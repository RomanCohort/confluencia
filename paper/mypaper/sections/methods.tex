\section{Methods}

\subsection{RNACTM pharmacokinetic model}

RNACTM models circRNA drug concentration through six compartments (subcutaneous depot, endosomal, cytosol, ER, extracellular vesicle, blood) via a linear ODE system $\dot{\mathbf{x}} = A\mathbf{x}$ with 14 rate constants. Because no circRNA drug PK dataset exists for parameter fitting, all rate constants are derived from literature-reported PK parameter ranges \citep{wesselhoeft2018,chen2019} via heuristic formulas that map micro-predictions (binding affinity, immune activation, inflammation) to biophysically plausible values: e.g., $k_a = 0.14 + 0.26 \times \text{binding}$, $k_d = 0.11 + 0.24 \times \text{immune}$. A 70/30 blend with a learned Random Forest residual corrects systematic bias where training data is available. The model outputs 72\,h concentration--time curves for five nucleotide modifications ($\Psi$, m6A, 5mC, I, unmodified) with endosomal escape efficiency of 1--5\% \citep{gilleron2013}.

\subsection{Sample-size-adaptive MOE ensemble}

Following the mixture-of-experts framework \citep{jacobs1991}, Confluencia selects expert architectures based on sample size: Ridge + HistogramGradientBoosting for $N<80$; adding RandomForest for $N<300$; adding MLP for $N\geq300$. Gating weights are computed as inverse-RMSE with biological constraints (membrane potential decay, lateral inhibition) that prevent expert collapse on small datasets. Epitope sequences are encoded by Mamba3Lite, a lightweight state-space model that captures long-range dependencies without quadratic attention cost.

\subsection{ADMET evaluation}

Drug candidates are evaluated for PAINS patterns, reactive functional groups, and rule-based ADMET flags from the 2083-dimension feature set (Morgan fingerprints + physicochemical descriptors). When data is insufficient for learned prediction, heuristic safety constraints enforce physiological floors: toxicity $\geq 0.06 + 0.16 \times \text{efficacy}$ and inflammation $\geq 0.05 + 0.12 \times \text{efficacy} \times \text{binding}$, preventing the system from reporting implausibly low risk for high-efficacy compounds.

\subsection{circRNA immunogenicity and microenvironment modeling}

Circular RNA secondary structure is predicted using ViennaRNA \citep{lorenz2011}, computing MFE folding and identifying double-stranded regions exceeding 33\,bp for PKR activation \citep{nallagatla2007}. Beyond structure, the circRNA module evaluates five innate immune sensing pathways (RIG-I, TLR3, TLR7, PKR, OAS) with nucleotide-modification-specific modulation factors---e.g., $\Psi$ reduces RIG-I and TLR sensing while preserving PKR detection of long dsRNA. The tumor microenvironment is characterized through immune cell deconvolution (22 cell types), Cancer-Immunity Cycle scoring \citep{chen2018immunity} across seven steps (antigen release $\rightarrow$ killing), and immune evasion assessment (PD-L1, MHC downregulation). Gene signatures (TROP2, Nectin-4, LIV1, B7H4, TMEM65) enable TNBC subtype-specific evaluation. This integrated immunogenicity--microenvironment assessment is unique to Confluencia: no existing tool connects circRNA modification effects to immune pathway activation and TME context.

\subsection{Uncertainty-adaptive five-dimension evaluation}

The joint score aggregates five dimensions (Clinical, Binding, Kinetics, Gene Signature, circRNA) with base weights $(0.30, 0.20, 0.15, 0.15, 0.20)$. Critically, each weight is modulated by a quadratic uncertainty penalty: $w_i' = w_i \times (1 - u_i)^2$, where $u_i$ is the per-dimension prediction uncertainty. This ensures that under-constrained dimensions (e.g., clinical outcomes with no training data) contribute near-zero weight rather than injecting noise. The composite score yields Go ($\geq0.65$), Conditional ($\geq0.40$), or No-Go recommendations, with a safety override that forces No-Go when safety penalty exceeds 0.30.
