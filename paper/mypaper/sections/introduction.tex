\section{Introduction}

Therapeutic circRNA offers advantages over linear mRNA in stability and tunable immunogenicity \citep{wesselhoeft2018,chen2019}. Yet circRNA drug development faces a tooling gap: existing computational platforms address small-molecule pharmacology (NetMHCpan for MHC binding \citep{reynisson2020}; ADMETlab for toxicity \citep{xiong2024}; Monolix for population PK) but none evaluates circRNA-specific dimensions---nucleotide modification effects on half-life ($\Psi$: ${\sim}15$\,h vs unmodified: 6.24\,h), circRNA immunogenicity pathways, translation potential, or miRNA sponge activity. Researchers must manually integrate outputs from unrelated tools, with no framework for producing unified Go/No-Go decisions.

Confluencia fills this gap with three design choices (Fig~\ref{fig:overview}): (1) RNACTM, a six-compartment ODE model for circRNA PK; (2) sample-size-adaptive Mixture-of-Experts \citep{jacobs1991} for binding prediction; (3) uncertainty-adaptive five-dimension evaluation yielding integrated Go/Conditional/No-Go recommendations that guide wet-lab prioritization.
