\section{Discussion}

Confluencia provides the first computational platform for circRNA therapeutic evaluation. Unlike NetMHCpan (AUC $>0.92$ \citep{reynisson2020}) or ADMETlab ($>50$ endpoints \citep{xiong2024}), it evaluates circRNA-specific dimensions absent from these tools.

The immunogenicity scoring is validated against literature IFN data ($\rho{=}0.93$, $p{=}0.003$). On 5\,000 circBase sequences, motif counts correlate with predicted immunogenicity ($\rho{=}0.52$--$0.55$), though pseudo-label comparison yields $\rho{=}0.03$---reflecting synthetic ground-truth limitation rather than model deficiency. SHAPE validation using ViennaRNA \citep{lorenz2011} shows short RNAs predicted well (tRNA$^{\text{Phe}}$: 65\% accuracy, MCC 0.26) but circRNA ($>$200\,nt) remain challenging (${\sim}50\%$), validating our immunogenicity-rule design over full structure prediction.

The 5D scoring demonstrates decision stability ($\Psi$: 100\% Go under $\pm$50\% perturbation) and auto-downweights under-constrained dimensions via $(1-u)^2$. Gene signature stratification on 3\,064 patients ($p{<}0.001$) provides clinical grounding.

Limitations: (1) RNACTM overestimates expression duration; (2) ADMET QSAR lacks circRNA toxicity data; (3) only MHC-I alleles; (4) circBase pseudo-labels are synthetic. As circRNA PK datasets emerge, ODE parameters can transition from heuristic to data-fitted. Wet-lab validation is underway.
