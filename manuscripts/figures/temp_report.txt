Investigation of Its Mechanism of  Action Objective: To address the clinical challenge that triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC) lacks highly effective and specific therapeutic strategies and exhibits marked heterogeneity in immune responses, we aimed to construct a candidate therapeutic tumor vaccine based   on circular RNA (circRNA) and to systematically evaluate the rationale for candidate target selection, antigen expression capacity, in vitro immune activation effects, in vivo antitumor activity, and its preliminary mechanism of action. Methods: First, GEPIA3 and the TISCH single-cell database were employed, in combination with cBioPortal, Kaplan–Meier Plotter, The Human Protein Atlas (HPA), and DeepLoc,   to perform candidate target screening, tumor-cell specificity validation, and clinical translational evaluation for TACSTD2 (TROP2), VTCN1 (B7-H4), NECTIN4, and SLC39A6 (LIV-1). Subsequently, cytotoxic T lymphocyte (CTL) epitope prediction and optimization were conducted under the HLA-A*02:01 background, and AlphaFold3 was used   to   simulate   the   structures   of   epitope–HLA   complexes.   Candidate   circRNA  plasmids were then constructed, followed by in vitro transcription and validation of circularization efficiency. A dendritic cell (DC)–T cell coculture system was established using   peripheral   blood   mononuclear   cell   (PBMC)-derived   DCs,   and   immunogenicity and cytotoxic activity were evaluated by ELISPOT, flow cytometry, Western blotting, and Calcein AM release assays. In addition, the in vivo antitumor efficacy and changes   in the tumor immune microenvironment were preliminarily assessed in a 4T1/TROP2 tumor-bearing mouse model. Results: Multi-omics and single-cell analyses demonstrated that TROP2, B7-H4, NECTIN4, and LIV-1 exhibited favorable tumor relevance and cellular specificity in TNBC. The combined coverage of these four targets reached 98% in the TCGA-TNBC cohort, and   their   high   expression   was   associated   with   shorter   recurrence-free   survival.   Based   on  these four candidate target proteins, a total of 17 candidate CTL epitopes were predicted, and AlphaFold3   simulations   suggested   that   their overall   binding   conformations   with HLA-A*02:01 were stable. These 17 candidate CTL epitopes were individually   constructed   as   circRNA   vaccines   for   functional   preliminary   screening.   The   results showed that the constructed circRNA vaccines displayed high circularization efficiency   and could effectively express antigens in DCs. Furthermore, the top two CTL epitopes   with the highest immunogenicity from each target protein were integrated to construct a multiepitope Combined circRNA vaccine for subsequent functional validation. In vitro experiments showed that the Combined circRNA vaccine promoted DC maturation, as indicated by an increased proportion of CD80/CD86 double-positive cells, enhanced   CD8+ T-cell activation (CD69+), maintained the costimulatory phenotype (CD28+), and promoted differentiation toward an effector memory-related phenotype (CD45RO+CCR7−). Animal experiments further demonstrated that the Combined group delayed   tumor   growth   and   increased   intratumoral   CD8+   T-cell   infiltration   and  Granzyme B expression. Conclusion: This study supports the feasibility of circRNA as a therapeutic vaccine platform for TNBC through a continuous chain of evidence spanning multidimensional target screening, epitope optimization, circRNA construction, and in vitro/in vivo functional validation. It also suggests that multiepitope combination design may represent an important strategy for enhancing tumor immunogenicity and in vivo antitumor efficacy.  5 ′  端与  3 ′  端游离末端，因此通常表现出更高的外切酶耐受性和更长的分子稳定性 [8–11] 。既往研究表明，经合理设计与纯化的外源  circRNA 可通过内部核糖体进入位点 （ internal   ribosome   entry   site ,  IRES ） 或  N6- 甲基腺苷 （ N6-methyladenosine, m6A ）相关机制实现非帽依赖翻译，并在一定条件下表现出较长的翻译持续时间 [8–10] 。与此同时， circRNA    平台在抗原表达持续性、构建灵活 性以及递送系统适配性方面均展现出良好的开发前景；近期综述亦进一步强调，  c i rc R N A   疫苗在抗原设计、体外合成、纯化和递送方面的快速进展，正推动其由概 念验证走向更具转化意义的癌症免疫治疗平台 [ 11 ] 。此外，最新研究提示，肿瘤来源  c i rc R N A   所编码的隐匿肽段本身亦可能参与抗肿瘤免疫应答塑造 [ 12 ]   ， 为  c i rc R N A   在肿瘤免疫治疗中的应用提供了新的理论依据。 基于上述背景，本研究围绕 “ 候选靶点筛选 — 表位优选 —circRNA   构建 — 体外 免疫验证 — 体内药效与机制初探 ” 的技术路线展开，重点回答以下三个问题：其 一，如何在  TNBC   中筛选兼具肿瘤相关性、细胞特异性、临床可及性及转化潜力的候选抗原；其二， circRNA  平台能否支持候选抗原的稳定表达并诱导抗原特异性 细胞免疫；其三，多表位联合策略是否较单表位构建更有利于提高体外免疫原性及 体内抗肿瘤活性。通过对上述问题的系统分析，本研究旨在为  circRNA   治疗性肿 瘤疫苗在  TNBC  中的进一步开发提供实验依据和方法学参考。
