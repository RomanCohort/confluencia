\section{Results}

\subsection{RNACTM validation against literature}

Half-life predictions match published values within 4.1\% for three modifications with available data: unmodified (6.24\,h vs 6.0\,h), $\Psi$ (15.61\,h vs 15.0\,h), and m6A (11.24\,h vs 10.8\,h) \citep{wesselhoeft2018,chen2019}. 5mC and I modifications lack published circRNA half-life data, precluding validation. Endosomal escape fraction (4.43\% simulated) falls within the 1--5\% range reported by Gilleron et al. \citep{gilleron2013}. Protein expression duration is overestimated (97\,h simulated vs ${\sim}48$\,h reported), indicating the clearance model needs refinement---a known limitation of literature-derived rather than data-fitted parameters.

\subsection{Small-sample prediction}

On epitope efficacy prediction ($N{=}300$, 5-fold CV), the MOE ensemble achieves 39.2\% MAE reduction over Ridge ($p<0.001$) and 4.9\% over HGB, confirming that adaptive gating benefits the small-sample regime. For drug efficacy with compact features ($d{=}154$), Ridge suffices ($R^2$ 0.984 vs MOE 0.982), consistent with the expectation that MOE complexity is unnecessary when data is sufficient and feature space is low-dimensional.

\subsection{Cross-module case study}

Three circRNA--drug pairs were evaluated through the full pipeline (Table~\ref{tab:casestudy}):
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{$\Psi$-circRNA + Aspirin}: Extended half-life (${\sim}15$\,h) with low ADMET risk yields a Go or Conditional recommendation---consistent with $\Psi$'s known stability advantage.
\item \textbf{Unmodified circRNA + Rhodanine}: Short half-life (6.24\,h), high immune activation, and PAINS scaffold trigger a No-Go with safety override---consistent with Rhodanine's known pan-assay interference.
\item \textbf{5mC-circRNA + Ibuprofen}: Intermediate half-life (${\sim}8$\,h) and moderate ADMET profile produce mixed signals, yielding a Conditional recommendation reflecting the 5D trade-off.
\end{itemize}

These recommendations emerge from cross-module reasoning---e.g., PK extending half-life while immunogenicity raises risk---that no single specialized tool can produce, as existing tools lack the circRNA-specific dimensions entirely.
