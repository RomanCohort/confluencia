\section{Methods}

\subsection{RNACTM pharmacokinetic model}

RNACTM simulates circRNA concentration through six compartments (Injection $\rightarrow$ lipid nanoparticle (LNP) $\rightarrow$ Endosome $\rightarrow$ Cytoplasmic RNA $\rightarrow$ Translated Protein $\rightarrow$ Clearance) via $\dot{\mathbf{x}} = A\mathbf{x}$ with six rate constants and late-phase accelerated clearance \citep{liu2023}. Rate constants are computed from literature ranges \citep{wesselhoeft2018,chen2019,hassett2019,gilleron2013} with modification-specific adjustments; late-phase accelerated clearance models proteasomal upregulation after ${\sim}48$\,h (Supplementary Table~S1).

\subsection{Small-molecule evaluation}

Expert architectures are selected by sample size \citep{jacobs1991}: Ridge + HGB ($N<80$); adding RF ($N<300$); adding MLP ($N\geq300$), with inverse-RMSE gating. ESM-2 \citep{lin2023} produced severe overfitting at $N<300$; sklearn models provide stable predictions without GPU dependency. Epitope sequences use Mamba3Lite encoding \citep{gu2023}; MHC features cover 153 class-I and 37 class-II alleles with allele-specific pseudo-sequence encoding; 56 additional alleles use allele-agnostic features due to insufficient per-allele samples (total 246 alleles). ADMET screening covers PAINS \citep{baell2010}, reactive groups, and safety floors preventing unsafe candidates.

\subsection{circRNA functional assessment}

The circRNA module covers structure, immunogenicity, and functional interactions (Table~\ref{tab:coverage}). ViennaRNA \citep{lorenz2011} predicts secondary structure; dsRNA $>$33\,bp flags protein kinase R (PKR) activation. Five innate immune pathways (RIG-I, TLR3, TLR7, PKR, OAS) receive modification-specific scoring. Functional axes cover miRNA sponge, RBP binding, translation potential, modification site prediction, and RNA--ligand docking. TNBC subtype signatures and immune deconvolution characterize the tumor microenvironment.

\subsection{Uncertainty-adaptive five-dimension evaluation}

Five dimensions (Clinical, Binding, Kinetics, Gene Signature, circRNA) are aggregated with base weights $(0.30, 0.20, 0.15, 0.15, 0.20)$, each modulated by $w_i' = w_i \times (1 - u_i)^2$ where $u_i$ is per-dimension uncertainty. Under-constrained dimensions auto-downweight. The composite yields Go ($\geq0.65$), Conditional ($\geq0.40$), or No-Go, with safety override at penalty $>0.30$.

\subsection{Closed-loop molecular evolution}

Evaluation outputs feed back into molecular optimization: ED2Mol generates candidate molecules/sequences; MOE predicts multi-objective scores (efficacy, binding, toxicity); risk gating filters candidates above adaptive toxicity thresholds; elite retention selects top-$k$ candidates for the next round. circRNA-specific operations preserve the back-splice junction (BSJ), optimize IRES motifs, rearrange UTRs, and switch nucleotide modifications---each directly affecting RNACTM rate constants (Supplementary Table~S5).
