\section{Results}

\subsection{Functional coverage}

Confluencia covers circRNA-specific dimensions absent from existing tools and provides the only cross-modality evaluation (Table~\ref{tab:coverage}).

\subsection{RNACTM dynamics}

Half-life consistency with parametrization sources is by design within numerical precision ($<$4.1\%); this constitutes consistency verification, not independent validation. The informative deviation is the expression window (40\,h vs ${\sim}48$\,h, 17\%). Endosomal escape (5.2\%) slightly exceeds the 1--5\% range \citep{gilleron2013}, reflecting range uncertainty.

\subsection{Small-sample ensemble}

On epitope efficacy ($N{=}300$, 5-fold CV), MOE achieves R$^2{=}0.82$, MAE${=}0.39$ ($p{<}0.001$), 39\% MAE reduction over Ridge. On 91\,K drug data (6 targets), MOE achieves R$^2{=}0.60$ for efficacy and R$^2{=}0.95$ for target binding; the high binding R$^2$ reflects that molecular binding is well-captured by physicochemical features, while efficacy is inherently harder. Removing 2048-dim Morgan fingerprints improves binding R$^2$ from 0.67 to 0.95 (Supplementary Table~S2).

\subsection{MHC binding}

On 288\,K IEDB peptides, allele-specific features achieve AUC${=}0.80$ ($+$0.06 over allele-agnostic). On 52\,K binary data (246 alleles), overall AUC${=}0.83$. Per-allele performance varies: focused-repertoire alleles reach AUC${>}0.92$ (NetMHCpan-level); common alleles with broad peptide diversity remain challenging (A*02:01: 0.67) (Table~\ref{tab:allele_auc}).

\subsection{Evaluation stability}

Under $\pm$50\% weight perturbations, decisions remain stable: $\Psi$ 100\% Go, unmodified 100\% Conditional. Kinetics is most critical---removing it shifts $\Psi$ from Go to Conditional ($\Delta{-4.6\%}$). Immunogenicity rankings agree with literature IFN data ($\rho{=}0.93$, direction consistency over 5 conditions; not quantitative validation).

\subsection{Gene signature}

Five-gene scorer stratifies 3\,064 patients: log-rank $p{<}0.001$, high-target vs low-target death rate 57\% vs 20\% (C-index 0.52, exploratory).

\subsection{Cross-modality prioritization}
