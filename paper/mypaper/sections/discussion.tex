\section{Discussion}

Confluencia provides a computational platform for integrated circRNA therapeutic evaluation. Unlike NetMHCpan (AUC $>0.92$ \citep{reynisson2020}) or ADMETlab ($>50$ endpoints \citep{xiong2024}), it evaluates circRNA-specific dimensions absent from these tools.

Three lines of evidence support the platform: (1) circRNA immunogenicity scoring is consistent with literature IFN data ($\rho{=}0.93$, $p{=}0.003$); (2) 5D decisions remain stable under $\pm$50\% weight perturbation ($\Psi$: 100\% Go), with kinetics as the most critical dimension; (3) gene signature target availability stratifies 3\,064 patients by tertiles (log-rank $p{<}0.001$, high vs low death rate 57\% vs 20\%) with biological plausibility---high target expression correlates with longer survival in TNBC, consistent with these genes being therapeutic targets. Under-constrained dimensions auto-downweight via $(1-u)^2$, reflecting data limitations.

RNACTM pharmacokinetic predictions are consistent with published half-lives within 4.1\% for unmodified, m6A, and $\Psi$-modified circRNA \citep{wesselhoeft2018,chen2019}. The productive expression window (time with viable cytoplasmic RNA and detectable protein) aligns with literature after incorporating late-phase accelerated clearance. MHC binding prediction covers 173 MHC-I alleles (HLA-A/B/C) and 37 MHC-II alleles (DRB1/DQ/DP), enabling CD4$^+$ T-cell epitope screening alongside CD8$^+$.

Limitations: (1) RNACTM rate constants are derived from literature priors---no circRNA-specific PK datasets exist for independent fitting; (2) immunogenicity consistency checking uses the same literature that informed scoring rules; (3) gene signature stratifies patients by target availability (high vs low tertile: 57\% vs 20\% death rate), but C-index is modest (0.52), reflecting that the score captures therapeutic target availability rather than intrinsic disease risk; (4) circRNA ADMET maps classical endpoints to circRNA analogs rather than direct experimental toxicology data; (5) only half-life and immunogenicity among five circRNA functional dimensions have been benchmarked---translation potential, miRNA sponge, and RBP binding lack independent evaluation. Wet-lab validation is underway.
