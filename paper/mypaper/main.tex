\begin{abstract}
\textbf{Summary:} CircRNA therapeutics require evaluation of nucleotide modification effects on half-life, immunogenicity, and translation---dimensions absent from existing drug evaluation tools. Confluencia integrates RNACTM, a six-compartment circRNA pharmacokinetic model (half-life validated within 4.1\% against published data), sample-size-adaptive mixture-of-experts for binding prediction (39.2\% MAE reduction at $N{=}300$), and uncertainty-adaptive five-dimension scoring that auto-downweights under-constrained dimensions. The platform evaluates circRNA functional interactions (miRNA/RBP binding, translation potential, RNA modification sites) alongside small-molecule ADMET, producing Go/Conditional/No-Go recommendations that guide wet-lab prioritization---e.g., $\Psi$ and m6A modifications are prioritized for synthesis while unmodified circRNA is deprioritized due to short half-life and high immune activation.\\
\textbf{Availability and implementation:} \url{https://github.com/RomanCohort/confluencia}, MIT license. Python 3.8+, Linux/macOS/Windows.\\
\textbf{Contact:} \email{18806370529@163.com}
\end{abstract}
