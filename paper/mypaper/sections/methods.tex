\section{Methods}

\subsection*{RNACTM compartment simulation}
RNACTM models six compartments (endosome, cytoplasm, ribosome, protein, immune clearance, degradation) as a linear ODE system solved analytically. Rate constants from literature: unmodified half-life 6.24\,h \citep{wesselhoeft2018}, m6A 10.8\,h \citep{chen2019}, $\Psi$ 15.0\,h \citep{liu2023}. This is a deterministic literature-parameterized simulation, not a population PK model; it provides compartment dynamics without inter-individual variability. Sensitivity: $\pm$20\% rate variation changes half-life by $\leq$8.2\%. Runs in $<$1\,s on laptop CPU.

\subsection*{Drug and epitope pipelines}
Drug: sample-size-adaptive weighted model averaging (WMA; inverse-RMSE weighting, a stacking simplification \citep{jacobs1991}) switches from Morgan FP+XGBoost to simpler features+Ridge for N$<$2\,000; 91K ChEMBL data across 6 targets. ADMET screening via ADMETlab \citep{xiong2024} with PAINS \citep{baell2010}. Epitope: Mamba3Lite \citep{gu2023} encoding + allele-aware MHC features (MHC-I: 1\,018 dims; MHC-II: 947 dims via NetMHCIIpan pseudo-sequences); 288K IEDB data, 5-fold CV. Both pipelines CPU-only; no GPU required.

\subsection*{circRNA functional assessment}
Five innate immune pathways scored from sequence motifs (RIG-I, TLR7, PKR, OAS, MDA5). Pathway weights (e.g.\ RIG-I 35/40/20/5\%) are author-informed heuristics, not empirically calibrated. Structure via ViennaRNA \citep{lorenz2011}. Five functional axes: half-life/modification, immunogenicity, miRNA sponge, RBP binding, translation potential. Uncertainty: half-life and immunogenicity $u{=}0.15$; miRNA/RBP/translation $u{=}0.8$, yielding effective weights of $(1{-}0.8)^2{=}0.04$.

\subsection*{Five-dimension evaluation}
Dimensions: Clinical (drug, 30\%), Binding (epitope, 20\%), Kinetics (PK, 15\%), Gene Signature (TCGA/METABRIC, 15\%; C-index 0.52, flagged exploratory, $u{=}0.50$), circRNA (20\%). Effective weights incorporate $(1{-}u_i)^2$. Go/Conditional/No-Go thresholds: $\geq$0.65/0.35--0.65/$<$0.35. Safety override: genotoxicity risk $>$0.7 forces No-Go regardless of composite.
