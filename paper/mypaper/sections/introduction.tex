\section{Introduction}

Therapeutic circular RNA (circRNA) offers advantages over linear mRNA in stability and tunable immunogenicity \citep{wesselhoeft2018,chen2019}. In our circRNA therapeutic development program, we found that existing computational platforms address small-molecule pharmacology---NetMHCpan for major histocompatibility complex (MHC) binding \citep{reynisson2020}, ADMETlab for absorption, distribution, metabolism, excretion, and toxicity (ADMET) screening \citep{xiong2024}, Monolix \citep{dumont2014} for population pharmacokinetics---but none evaluates circRNA-specific dimensions: nucleotide modification effects on half-life ($\Psi$: ${\sim}15$\,h vs unmodified: 6.24\,h), circRNA immunogenicity pathways, IRES-mediated translation potential, or microRNA (miRNA) sponge activity. We developed Confluencia to integrate these evaluations in a single platform, producing unified Go/Conditional/No-Go decisions that guide our wet-lab prioritization.

Three design choices address the data-limited setting (Fig~\ref{fig:overview}): (1) RNACTM, a six-compartment ordinary differential equation (ODE) model for circRNA pharmacokinetics (PK); (2) sample-size-adaptive Mixture-of-Experts (MOE) \citep{jacobs1991} for binding prediction; (3) uncertainty-adaptive five-dimension (5D) evaluation that auto-downweights under-constrained dimensions.
