\begin{abstract}
Confluencia: a computational framework for circRNA therapeutic candidate screening with tumor microenvironment (TME) simulation integration. Integrates immunogenicity scoring, multi-cell-type TME dynamics, drug combination analysis, patient stratification, and adaptive dosing guidance. \textbf{Key features:} (1) RIG-I scoring constrained to dsRNA backbone pathway (circRNA lacks 5' termini); (2) TME simulation with 10 cell types and spatial heterogeneity; (3) Multi-method synergy scoring (Bliss, Loewe, HSA, CI) with interpretation guidance; (4) Six-subtype patient stratification; (5) Adaptive dosing algorithm. \textbf{Validation status:} Direction consistency with literature ($N=7$ exact, CI 0.50-0.99); expanded validation planned ($N$≥30, Q3 2026--Q1 2027). \textbf{Positioning:} Research screening tool, not validated clinical predictor.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Therapeutic circRNA offers stability advantages over linear mRNA \citep{wesselhoeft2018,chen2019}, yet no existing platform provides integrated screening for circRNA immunotherapy candidates. LinearDesign optimizes codons for linear mRNA; NetMHCpan predicts MHC binding; ADMETlab assesses small-molecule toxicity. None addresses circRNA-specific immunogenicity pathways or TME dynamics.

Confluencia provides: (1) \textbf{RIG-I constraint:} circRNA lacks 5'/3' termini, eliminating canonical blunt-end recognition---constrained to dsRNA backbone pathway only; (2) ViennaRNA MFE weighting (40\%) capturing stability-immunogenicity relationship; (3) \textbf{TME simulation:} Agent-based model for immunotherapy response dynamics; (4) \textbf{Combination analysis:} Multi-method synergy scoring with interpretation guidance; (5) \textbf{Patient stratification:} Six immune phenotypes for hypothesis generation; (6) \textbf{Adaptive dosing:} Algorithmic framework for future validation.

\textbf{Scope clarification:} This framework is positioned as a \textit{candidate screening tool} for research hypothesis generation, not a validated clinical predictor. All scoring weights and thresholds are author-informed heuristics pending empirical calibration against patient outcome data.

\section{Methods}
\textbf{Immunogenicity scoring:} Five pathways---RIG-I (40\%), TLR7 (20\%), TLR8 (15\%), PKR (30\%)---evaluated from sequence motifs. \textbf{Weight disclaimer:} These weights are heuristics derived from literature review (dominant dsRNA drivers \citep{zhang2016,nallagatla2007}), not empirically calibrated against clinical outcomes. \textbf{Sensitivity analysis:} Perturbation of pathway weights by $\pm$5\%, $\pm$10\%, $\pm$20\% yields ranking changes of $\leq$8\%, $\leq$15\%, $\leq$28\% respectively (see Supplementary Material Table S2). At $\pm$20\% perturbation, decision categories (Go/Conditional/No-Go) remain stable for 85\% of test sequences ($N=50$). Users should interpret scores as relative ranking, not absolute prediction. For $\Psi$/m1$\Psi$ modifications, divide TLR scores by $\sim$10 \citep{anderson2019,kariko2005}.

\textbf{TME Simulation:} Agent-based model with 10 cell types (CD8$^+$ T, CD4$^+$ T, Treg, B, NK, M1/M2, MDSC, CAF, tumor) across 3 compartments (core/margin/stroma). Cytokine network: IFN-$\gamma$, TNF-$\alpha$, IL-2, IL-6, IL-10, TGF-$\beta$. \textbf{Biological limitations acknowledged (see Discussion for consolidated limitations):}
\begin{itemize}
\item Dendritic cells (DCs) not modeled---circRNA vaccine priming via DC cross-presentation pathway is conceptualized but not simulated
\item Type I IFNs (IFN-$\alpha$/IFN-$\beta$) not explicitly modeled---RIG-I activation to IFN production cascade represented implicitly in immunogenicity score
\item TLR structural accessibility not implemented---ViennaRNA used for RIG-I only; TLR motif accessibility in stem vs loop not distinguished (see Supplementary Material Section 2)
\item IL-12 not modeled---affects Th1 polarization predictions
\end{itemize}
Dynamics example:
\begin{equation}
\frac{d[T_{CD8}]}{dt} = \alpha_{act}[APC] \cdot f(IFN\gamma) - \beta_{exh}[T_{CD8}] \cdot g(PD\text{-}1 \times PD\text{-}L1)
\end{equation}
Horizon: 168h, timestep: 1h. Classification: Hot ($>$70\% immune score), Cold ($<$30\%), Excluded, Immunosuppressive, High-target, Mixed.

\textbf{Integrated Scoring:} TME simulation outputs contribute to the overall candidate score:
\begin{equation}
\text{Total Score} = 0.35 \times S_{immuno} + 0.30 \times S_{TME} + 0.20 \times S_{safety} + 0.15 \times S_{synergy}
\end{equation}
where $S_{TME}$ aggregates TME dynamics metrics with explicit normalization and floor protection:
\begin{equation}
S_{TME} = 0.4 \cdot \mathcal{N}_{CD8} + 0.3 \cdot \mathcal{N}_{Treg} + 0.2 \cdot \mathcal{N}_{M1M2} + 0.1 \cdot \mathcal{N}_{Tumor}
\end{equation}
with normalized components:
\begin{align}
\mathcal{N}_{CD8} &= \text{clip}\left(\frac{CD8_{final}}{\max(CD8_{initial}, 0.01)}, [0, 1]\right) \\
\mathcal{N}_{Treg} &= \text{clip}\left(1 - \frac{Treg}{\max(CD8_{final}, 0.01)}, [0, 1]\right) \\
\mathcal{N}_{M1M2} &= \text{clip}\left(\frac{M1}{\max(M2, 0.01)} \times \frac{1}{2.0}, [0, 1]\right) \\
\mathcal{N}_{Tumor} &= \frac{Tumor_{reduction}}{100}
\end{align}
\textbf{Mathematical safeguards:} Floor values (0.01) prevent division-by-zero in Cold TME scenarios (CD8$\rightarrow$0) or M2-depleted conditions. Clip functions bound each component to [0, 1] ensuring $S_{TME}$ range [0, 1]. \textbf{Weight disclaimer:} All coefficients are heuristic (see Supplementary Material Section 4 for derivation and sensitivity analysis). \textbf{Validation gap disclosure:} $S_{immuno}$ has direction consistency (r=0.91, N=7); $S_{TME}$ is unvalidated simulation output---30\% of Total Score derives from unvalidated components. TME metrics should be interpreted as relative ranking, not absolute prediction.

\textbf{Drug Combination:} 8 immunotherapy drugs with PK/PD parameters. Synergy methods: Bliss independence, Loewe additivity, HSA, Chou-Talalay Combination Index (CI). \textbf{Interpretation guidance:} For immunotherapy combinations where mechanisms interact (both affect T cells), Bliss independence assumption may not hold. \textbf{Recommendation categories:}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Recommended:} Bliss $>$0.10 and CI $<$1.0 (both metrics indicate synergy)
\item \textbf{Conditional:} Bliss $>$0.10 and CI 1.0--2.0 (synergy with dose considerations)
\item \textbf{Requires monitoring:} Bliss $>$0.10 and CI $>$2.0 (effect-level synergy but dose-level antagonism; requires dose optimization before clinical use)
\item \textbf{Not recommended:} Bliss $<$0 or CI $>$3.0 (antagonistic)
\end{itemize}
Example: circRNA + cyclophosphamide (Bliss=0.131, CI=2.67) falls into ``Requires monitoring''---shows independent-effect synergy but requires careful dose optimization due to dose-ratio antagonism.

\textbf{Patient Stratification:} Six subtypes based on TME phenotype: S1 (Hot, 70-100\% immune score), S2 (Cold, 0-30\%), S3 (Excluded, 30-50\%), S4 (Immunosuppressive, 20-40\%), S5 (High-target, 25-45\%), S6 (Mixed, 40-70\%). \textbf{Disclaimer:} Subtype thresholds (CD8 high $>$0.6, PD-L1 high $>$0.5) are heuristic, not ROC-optimized. Expected response ranges derived from simulation, not validated against patient outcomes.

\textbf{Adaptive Dosing:} Algorithmic framework with rules: response\_good (dose$\times$0.9), toxicity\_grade2 (dose$\times$0.8), toxicity\_grade3 (dose$\times$0.5). \textbf{Clinical limitation:} Immunotherapy kinetics differ from framework assumptions---pembrolizumab half-life 26 days vs circRNA 7 days; response assessment requires $\geq$12 weeks per RECIST 1.1 (not 4 weeks as in algorithm). Framework requires clinical validation before implementation.

\textbf{PK simulation:} 6-compartment ODE with half-lives: unmodified 6.24h \citep{wesselhoeft2018}, m6A 10.8h \citep{chen2019}, $\Psi$ 15.0h \citep{liu2023}. \textbf{Extrapolation note:} 5mC ($\sim$12.5h) and ms2m6A ($\sim$20h) are author estimates extrapolated from linear mRNA; circRNA-specific degradation may differ.

\section{Results}
\textbf{RIG-I evaluation:} 83.3\% direction consistency ($N=6$ test sequences). GC-rich dsRNA: 0.81; AU-rich: 0.34 despite 95.8\% paired---stability determines activation, confirming biological principle.

\textbf{Literature consistency:} Exact measurements ($N=7$): $r$=0.91 (95\% CI: 0.50--0.99, $p$=0.004). \textbf{Critical caveat:} CI width 0.49 reflects small sample uncertainty; result indicates \textit{direction consistency only}, not quantitative validation. Range estimates ($N=13$): 85\% direction agreement. m6A effect: 81\% experimental vs 57\% predicted---24\% deviation explained by: (1) context-dependent reader proteins (YTHDF1 vs YTHDF2); (2) position-specific effects (coding region vs IRES-proximal); (3) cell-type variability in reader expression. Model provides conservative estimate.

\textbf{TME Simulation:} 168h simulation demonstrates TME classification concept: Hot TME predicts higher checkpoint response (simulation-derived range 0.60-0.85) vs Cold TME (0.30-0.55). \textbf{Validation status:} Simulation outputs are model predictions, not validated against patient imaging data. CD8 expansion 1.68$\times$ with anti-PD-1 is simulation output for responsive patient profile. \textbf{TME Score distribution:} Across 50 simulated profiles, $S_{TME}$ ranged 0.32--0.78 (mean=0.54, SD=0.12). Hot TME profiles (S1) showed mean $S_{TME}$=0.71 vs Cold TME (S2) mean $S_{TME}$=0.38.

\textbf{Combination Analysis:} Top combinations: circRNA + pembrolizumab (Bliss=0.122, CI=2.67---Conditional), circRNA + cyclophosphamide (Bliss=0.131, CI=2.67---Requires monitoring). When metrics disagree, CI prioritized for dose-dependent interpretation.

\textbf{Stratification:} 50 simulated patients distributed: S1 (12\%), S2 (28\%), S3 (18\%), S4 (15\%), S5 (17\%), S6 (10\%). \textbf{Note:} Distribution reflects simulation inputs, not real patient data.

\textbf{Adaptive Dosing:} 12-week simulation (responsive profile): tumor 101$\rightarrow$42 (59\% reduction), dose 2.0$\rightarrow$1.65 mg/kg. \textbf{Demonstration purpose:} Shows algorithm behavior, not validated clinical outcome.

\textbf{Performance:} Immunogenicity: 85$\pm$23ms ($N=100$). TME simulation: 1.2s/168h. Full pipeline: $<$5min CPU.

\section{Discussion}
\textbf{Consolidated Limitations:} This framework has five major limitations that users must consider:

(1) \textbf{Validation gap:} Current validation ($N$=7) precludes quantitative prediction. Expanded validation ($N$≥30) planned with timeline: IRB approval (Q3 2026) $\rightarrow$ sample collection (Q3--Q4 2026) $\rightarrow$ IFN-ELISA analysis (Q1 2027) $\rightarrow$ results integration (Q2 2027).

(2) \textbf{Heuristic weights:} All pathway weights and scoring thresholds are literature-derived heuristics. Sensitivity analysis ($\pm$20\%) shows rankings are moderately robust but calibration against patient outcomes is required for clinical confidence.

(3) \textbf{Incomplete TME biology:} Missing components include: (a) dendritic cells (critical for antigen cross-presentation), (b) Type I IFN cascade (IFN-$\alpha$/IFN-$\beta$ from RIG-I activation), (c) IL-12 (Th1 polarization), (d) TLR structural accessibility. These omissions may affect prediction accuracy for certain TME types.

(4) \textbf{Cancer-type specificity:} TME parameters are generic; specific cancers (PDAC with desmoplastic barrier, glioblastoma with immune privilege, renal cell carcinoma with high baseline immunogenicity) may require parameter recalibration. Sensitivity analysis ($\pm$20\% on TME weights) shows 14\% classification instability, suggesting weights may not generalize across cancer types.

(5) \textbf{Adaptive dosing kinetics:} The algorithm assumes weekly dose adjustment, incompatible with checkpoint inhibitor pharmacokinetics (26-day half-life). Clinical implementation requires modification to monthly assessment intervals.

(6) \textbf{Scope limitation:} All evidence is literature-derived; no prospective experimental validation. Framework generates hypotheses for wet-lab testing, not clinical decisions.

\textbf{Positioning:} Research screening tool pending wet-lab validation. Not intended for clinical decision-making.
