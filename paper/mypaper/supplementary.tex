\subsection*{TME Simulation — Full Module Documentation}

This section documents the TME simulation module that is included in the Confluencia codebase. \textbf{Important:} This module was removed from the main manuscript scope to focus on validated core capabilities (immunogenicity scoring, PK simulation, 4D triage). It is retained in the codebase and supplementary for users who require mechanistic intuition alongside screening recommendations.

\paragraph{Architecture:} Agent-based model with 10 cell types (CD8$^+$ T, CD4$^+$ T, Treg, B, NK, M1/M2 macrophages, MDSC, CAF, tumor) across 3 spatial compartments (core, margin, stroma). Cytokine network: IFN-$\gamma$, TNF-$\alpha$, IL-2, IL-6, IL-10, TGF-$\beta$. Simulation horizon: 168h, timestep: 1h.

\paragraph{TME Score:}
\begin{equation}
S_{TME} = 0.4 \cdot \mathcal{N}_{CD8} + 0.3 \cdot \mathcal{N}_{Treg} + 0.2 \cdot \mathcal{N}_{M1M2} + 0.1 \cdot \mathcal{N}_{Tumor}
\end{equation}
with normalized components:
\begin{align}
\mathcal{N}_{CD8} &= \text{clip}\left(\frac{CD8_{final}}{\max(CD8_{initial}, 0.01)}, [0, 1]\right) \\
\mathcal{N}_{Treg} &= \text{clip}\left(1 - \frac{Treg}{\max(CD8_{final}, 0.01)}, [0, 1]\right) \\
\mathcal{N}_{M1M2} &= \text{clip}\left(\frac{M1}{\max(M2, 0.01)} \times \frac{1}{2.0}, [0, 1]\right) \\
\mathcal{N}_{Tumor} &= \frac{Tumor_{reduction}}{100}
\end{align}

\textbf{Mathematical safeguards:} Floor values (0.01) prevent division-by-zero in Cold TME scenarios. Clip functions bound each component to $[0, 1]$.

\paragraph{Classification:} Hot ($>$70\% immune score), Cold ($<$30\%), Excluded, Immunosuppressive, High-target, Mixed.

\paragraph{Biological limitations acknowledged:}
\begin{itemize}
\item Dendritic cells not modeled (circRNA vaccine priming via DC cross-presentation is conceptualized but not simulated)
\item Type I IFNs (IFN-$\alpha$/IFN-$\beta$) not modeled (RIG-I activation cascade represented implicitly in immunogenicity score)
\item IL-12 not modeled (affects Th1 polarization predictions)
\item TLR structural accessibility not implemented
\item TME parameters are generic; specific cancers (PDAC, glioblastoma, RCC) may require recalibration
\item All weights are heuristic; TME Score is unvalidated simulation output
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Limitation disclosure:} $S_{TME}$ has zero experimental validation. It should be interpreted as a relative ranking for hypothesis generation, not an absolute prediction. Users should treat TME scores as exploratory metrics pending external validation against patient biopsy data (IHC for CD8, Treg, M1/M2).

\subsection*{Table S2: Immunogenicity scoring — circRNA-specific design}

\small IMPORTANT: circRNA is a covalently closed loop with NO 5' or 3' ends. The canonical RIG-I pathway (5'-triphosphate blunt-end recognition; Schlee et al., 2009) does NOT apply. circRNA activates RIG-I through dsRNA backbone structures formed by inverted repeat sequences (Zhang et al., Nat Immunol 2016). RIG-I scoring uses ViennaRNA structure prediction with MFE stability scoring (40\% weight), emphasizing that dsRNA \textbf{stability}---not mere presence---determines RIG-I activation. Pathway weights (RIG-I 0.35, TLR7 0.20, TLR8 0.15, PKR 0.30) are author-informed heuristics, NOT empirically calibrated; $\pm$0.05 perturbation changes overall by $\leq$4.2\%.

\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
\textbf{Pathway} & \textbf{Weight} & \textbf{circRNA mechanism} \\
\midrule
RIG-I & 0.35 & dsRNA backbone with MFE stability weighting (ViennaRNA) \\
TLR7 & 0.20 & GU-rich ssRNA motifs in loops/bulges \\
TLR8 & 0.15 & AU-rich ssRNA motifs (distinct from TLR7) \\
PKR & 0.30 & dsRNA regions $>$33bp \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\textbf{RIG-I scoring improvement:} Uses ViennaRNA 2.7+ for MFE structure prediction. The score formula emphasizes MFE stability (40\% weight):
$$S_{\text{RIG-I}} = 0.20 \cdot f_{\text{dsRNA}} + 0.40 \cdot S_{\text{MFE}} + 0.20 \cdot f_{\text{GC}} \cdot f_{\text{dsRNA}} + 0.15 \cdot L_{\text{stem}} + 0.05 \cdot N_{\text{stems}}$$

Key biological insight: AU-pairing forms dsRNA structure (95.8\% paired in AU-rich sequences), but AU-rich dsRNA has low stability (MFE $\approx -0.5$\,kcal/mol/nt) and low RIG-I activation, while GC-rich dsRNA is stable (MFE $\approx -1.3$\,kcal/mol/nt) with high RIG-I activation. This correctly predicts that AU-rich circRNA has low immunogenicity despite forming dsRNA structures.

\subsection*{Table S3: m6A effect directionality}

\small m6A effect on immunogenicity is context-dependent, not uniformly immune evasion. The reader protein context and modification site distribution determine the net effect:

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep,topsep=0pt]
\item Coding region m6A $\to$ YTHDF2-mediated degradation $\to$ reduced RIG-I recognition (Chen et al., 2019)
\item IRES-proximal m6A $\to$ YTHDF1-mediated translation enhancement $\to$ potentially increased immune visibility (Meyer, 2015)
\item m6A reader context (YTHDF1 vs YTHDF2) and cell-type effects NOT modeled
\item Net effect: up to 30\% reduction OR up to 15\% increase in immunogenicity, depending on site context
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Pareto front selection algorithm:} Non-dominated sorting with crowding distance tiebreaker, conceptually similar to NSGA-II \citep{deb2002}. Four objectives are optimized simultaneously: efficacy (maximize), toxicity (minimize), immunogenicity (minimize for therapeutics; for vaccine applications, an optimal intermediate value is targeted to balance immunogenicity with safety), and stability (maximize). A solution $\mathbf{x}$ dominates solution $\mathbf{y}$ ($\mathbf{x} \prec \mathbf{y}$) if $\mathbf{x}$ is no worse than $\mathbf{y}$ in all objectives and strictly better in at least one objective. The Pareto front is identified by iteratively extracting non-dominated solutions from the candidate pool. When multiple solutions occupy the same Pareto rank, crowding distance is used as a secondary sorting criterion to promote diversity: solutions in less-crowded regions of objective space are prioritized.
