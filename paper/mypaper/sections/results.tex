\subsection{RNACTM pharmacokinetics}

Half-life predictions match published values within 4.1\% (Supplementary Table~S1). Endosomal escape (5.2\%) falls within the 1--5\% literature range \citep{gilleron2013}; the productive expression window (40\,h) is within 17\% of the published ${\sim}48$\,h range \citep{wesselhoeft2018}.

\subsection{Small-sample ensemble}

On epitope efficacy ($N{=}300$, 5-fold CV), adaptive MOE achieves R$^2{=}0.82$ (MAE${=}0.39$), reducing MAE 39\% over Ridge and 5--26\% over non-linear baselines \citep{pedregosa2011}. On drug efficacy ($N{=}200$, 154 features), MOE achieves R$^2{=}0.982$, comparable to Ridge while improving MAE 7.5--52.7\% over non-linear baselines (Supplementary Table~S2).

\subsection{MHC binding prediction}

On 288\,K IEDB peptides, MHC-aware training with allele-specific features (1018-dim MHC-I + 947-dim MHC-II pseudo-sequence encoding) achieves overall AUC${=}0.80$, a ${+}0.06$ improvement over the allele-agnostic baseline (AUC${=}0.74$, 317 features). Table~\ref{tab:allele_auc} shows per-allele AUC on 52\,K IEDB binary binding data (246 alleles, HGB). Performance varies substantially: well-characterized alleles with focused peptide repertoires (HLA-A*33:01, A*33:03) reach AUC${>}0.92$, matching NetMHCpan-level accuracy, whereas common alleles with broad peptide diversity (HLA-A*02:01, A*11:01) remain challenging (AUC${<}0.70$). This heterogeneity reflects within-allele peptide diversity rather than training volume---HLA-A*02:01 binds peptides from diverse protein sources, making its binding groove harder to model from generic features alone. FeatureSpec auto-detects the MHC class from allele names and activates allele-specific encoding when an \texttt{mhc\_allele} column is present---no manual configuration required. Allele-aware mode is recommended for $N{\geq}5000$ datasets; the 317-dim allele-agnostic mode remains preferable for small-sample ($N{<}300$) settings where high-dimensional features risk overfitting (Supplementary Table~S6).

\subsection{Evaluation stability}

Under 1\,000 $\pm$50\% weight perturbations, decisions remain stable: $\Psi$ 100\% Go, unmodified 100\% Conditional, m6A 99.9\% (Supplementary Table~S4). Kinetics is the most critical dimension---removing it shifts $\Psi$ from Go to Conditional ($\Delta{-4.6\%}$). Immunogenicity scoring is consistent with literature IFN data ($\rho{=}0.93$, $p{=}0.003$): $\Psi$ lowest, unmodified highest \citep{chen2019}.

\subsection{Gene signature stratification}

The five-gene target availability scorer stratifies 3\,064 TCGA-BRCA \citep{weinstein2013} + METABRIC \citep{curtis2012} patients by tertiles: log-rank $p{<}0.001$, high-target vs low-target death rate 57\% vs 20\% (Supplementary Table~S3).

\subsection{Closed-loop molecular evolution}

ED2Mol+RL evolution over 5 rounds improves candidate quality: best reward 0.71$\rightarrow$0.95, average toxicity 0.42$\rightarrow$0.20, Pareto front 8$\rightarrow$27 solutions. Adaptive risk gating reduces high-toxicity candidates from 23\% to 3\% while maintaining efficacy (Supplementary Table~S5).
