\section{Introduction}

Therapeutic circRNA offers advantages over linear mRNA in stability, prolonged expression, and tunable immunogenicity \citep{wesselhoeft2018,chen2019}. Yet circRNA drug development faces a fundamental data barrier: unlike small molecules (ChEMBL, $>$2M assays) or linear mRNA (COVID vaccine PK datasets), circRNA therapeutics have no public pharmacokinetic datasets, no standardized clinical endpoints, and fewer than 300 labeled wet-lab samples.

This data vacuum means no specialized computational tool exists for circRNA drug evaluation. Researchers must cobble together generic predictors---NetMHCpan for MHC binding \citep{reynisson2020}, ADMETlab for toxicity \citep{xiong2024}, population PK software for small molecules---none of which model circRNA-specific dynamics such as nucleotide modification effects on half-life ($\Psi$: ${\sim}15$\,h vs unmodified: 6.24\,h) or circRNA immunogenicity--drug ADMET interplay.

Confluencia fills this gap with three data-scarcity-motivated design choices: (1) RNACTM, a six-compartment ODE model whose rate constants are computed from literature ranges rather than fitted from patient data; (2) a sample-size-adaptive Mixture-of-Experts ensemble \citep{jacobs1991} reducing model complexity when $N<300$; and (3) an uncertainty-adaptive five-dimension evaluation that auto-downweights under-constrained dimensions, preventing overconfident conclusions. Together, these enable cross-module reasoning that no single specialized tool can produce.
