\subsection*{Version history}

Confluencia v1.0 (2026-01) provided a monolithic frontend integrating GNN molecular prediction (five architectures: SimpleGNN, EnhancedGNN, PhysicsMessageGNN with Lennard-Jones/Coulomb potentials, E(3)-equivariant EGNN, multi-head GAT), PINN/PDE-constrained PK modeling (Fick's diffusion with Michaelis-Menten sink, CoeffNet for PDE coefficients), multiscale GNN-PINN cascade (atom-level GNN $\rightarrow$ GAT $\rightarrow$ PINN), cross-attention protein-ligand docking, ADMET screening, clinical trial simulation, and four-target gene signature scoring. v2.0 (2026-04) restructured into three independent modules (Drug, Epitope, circRNA) with a shared library, and added the circRNA-specific dimensions that define the current platform: RNACTM six-compartment PK, five innate immune pathway scoring, circRNA functional axes (miRNA sponge, RBP, translation, modifications, RNA docking), uncertainty-adaptive 5D evaluation, cross-modality Go/Conditional/No-Go decisions, and circRNA-specific evolutionary operations (BSJ protection, IRES optimization, UTR rearrangement, modification switching). v2.5 (2026-05) finalized module separation with bilingual UI support.

\subsection*{ESM-2 overfitting experiment}

Initial experiments used ESM-2 (650M parameter protein language model; Lin et al. 2023, Science 379:1121) for epitope binding prediction. On $N{=}300$ training samples, ESM-2 embeddings produced severe overfitting: training R$^2{>}0.95$ but validation R$^2{<}0.3$ across all 5 folds, with MAE increasing 2--3$\times$ from training to validation. This motivated the sample-size-adaptive MOE design using well-regularized sklearn models.
