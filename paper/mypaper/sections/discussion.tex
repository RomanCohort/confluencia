\section{Discussion}

Confluencia provides the first computational platform for circRNA therapeutic evaluation. Unlike NetMHCpan (AUC $>0.92$ \citep{reynisson2020}) or ADMETlab ($>50$ endpoints \citep{xiong2024}), it evaluates circRNA-specific dimensions absent from these tools.

Three validations support the platform: (1) circRNA immunogenicity scoring matches literature IFN data ($\rho{=}0.93$, $p{=}0.003$); (2) 5D decisions remain stable under $\pm$50\% weight perturbation ($\Psi$: 100\% Go), with kinetics as the most critical dimension; (3) gene signature stratification on 3\,064 patients ($p{<}0.001$, high-risk 57.3\% vs low-risk 27.3\% mortality) provides clinical grounding. Under-constrained dimensions auto-downweight via $(1-u)^2$, communicating limitations rather than hiding them.

RNACTM pharmacokinetic predictions match published half-lives within 4.1\% for unmodified, m6A, and $\Psi$-modified circRNA \citep{wesselhoeft2018,chen2019}. The productive expression window (time with viable cytoplasmic RNA and detectable protein) aligns with literature after incorporating late-phase accelerated clearance. MHC binding prediction covers 173 MHC-I alleles (HLA-A/B/C) and 37 MHC-II alleles (DRB1/DQ/DP), enabling CD4$^+$ T-cell epitope screening alongside CD8$^+$.

Limitations: (1) circRNA ADMET maps classical endpoints to circRNA analogs (immunogenicity, cytokine storm) rather than direct experimental toxicology data---as circRNA PK datasets emerge, parameters can transition from literature-derived priors to data-fitted; (2) encoder fallback mechanisms (ChemBERTa$\rightarrow$zeros, ESM-2$\rightarrow$AA composition) ensure offline operation but may reduce accuracy for novel sequences. Wet-lab validation is underway.
