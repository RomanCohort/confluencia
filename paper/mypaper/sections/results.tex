\subsection{RNACTM validation}

Half-life predictions match published values within 4.1\% \citep{wesselhoeft2018,chen2019}: unmodified (6.24 vs 6.0\,h), $\Psi$ (15.61 vs 15.0\,h), m6A (11.24 vs 10.8\,h). Endosomal escape (4.43\%) falls within 1--5\% \citep{gilleron2013}. The productive expression window (active translation from viable RNA) matches the published ${\sim}48$\,h range.

\subsection{Small-sample ensemble}

On epitope efficacy ($N{=}300$, 5-fold CV), adaptive MOE achieves 39.2\% MAE reduction over Ridge ($p<0.001$). On drug efficacy ($N{=}200$, 154 features), MOE achieves R$^2{=}0.982$ with 7.5--52.7\% MAE improvement over individual baselines \citep{pedregosa2011}.

\subsection{5D scoring robustness}

Under 1\,000 $\pm$50\% weight perturbations, decisions remain stable: $\Psi$ retains Go in 100\% of trials, unmodified retains Conditional in 100\%, m6A in 99.9\%. Kinetics is the most critical dimension---removing it flips $\Psi$ from Go to Conditional ($\Delta{-4.6\%}$).

\subsection{circRNA immunogenicity validation}

Pathway scores correlate strongly with literature IFN data ($\rho{=}0.93$, $p{=}0.003$, $n{=}7$ case studies): $\Psi$ shows lowest immunogenicity (IFN${=}0.15$), unmodified highest (IFN${=}0.85$), m6A intermediate (IFN${=}0.4$)---consistent with known modification effects \citep{chen2019}.

\subsection{Gene signature stratification}

The five-gene scorer stratifies 3\,064 TCGA+METABRIC patients: log-rank $p{<}0.001$, high-risk death rate 57.3\% vs low-risk 27.3\%, median survival 114 vs 42 months.

\subsection{Cross-modality prioritization}
