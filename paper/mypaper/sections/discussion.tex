\section{Discussion}

Confluencia provides a computational platform for integrated circRNA therapeutic evaluation. Unlike NetMHCpan (AUC $>0.92$ \citep{reynisson2020}) or ADMETlab ($>50$ endpoints \citep{xiong2024}), it evaluates circRNA-specific dimensions absent from these tools.

Three validations support the platform: (1) circRNA immunogenicity scoring is consistent with literature IFN data ($\rho{=}0.93$, $p{=}0.003$); (2) 5D decisions remain stable under $\pm$50\% weight perturbation ($\Psi$: 100\% Go), with kinetics as the most critical dimension; (3) gene signature target availability stratifies 3\,064 patients (log-rank $p{<}0.001$) with biological plausibility---high target expression correlates with longer survival in TNBC, consistent with these genes being therapeutic targets. Under-constrained dimensions auto-downweight via $(1-u)^2$, transparently reflecting data limitations.

RNACTM pharmacokinetic predictions match published half-lives within 4.1\% for unmodified, m6A, and $\Psi$-modified circRNA \citep{wesselhoeft2018,chen2019}. The productive expression window (time with viable cytoplasmic RNA and detectable protein) aligns with literature after incorporating late-phase accelerated clearance. MHC binding prediction covers 173 MHC-I alleles (HLA-A/B/C) and 37 MHC-II alleles (DRB1/DQ/DP), enabling CD4$^+$ T-cell epitope screening alongside CD8$^+$.

Limitations: (1) immunogenicity scoring was informed by the same literature used for consistency checking, precluding independent validation; (2) gene signature C-index is modest (0.52), and the stratification reflects therapeutic target availability rather than intrinsic disease risk; (3) circRNA ADMET maps classical endpoints to circRNA analogs rather than using direct experimental toxicology data---as circRNA PK datasets emerge, parameters can transition from literature-derived priors to data-fitted; (4) only half-life and immunogenicity among five circRNA functional dimensions have been validated---translation potential, miRNA sponge, and RBP binding lack independent benchmarks. Wet-lab validation is underway.
