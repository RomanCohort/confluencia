\section{Methods}

\subsection{RNACTM pharmacokinetic model}

RNACTM simulates circRNA concentration through six compartments (depot, endosomal, cytosol, ER, extracellular vesicle, blood) via $\dot{\mathbf{x}} = A\mathbf{x}$ with 14 rate constants \citep{liu2023}. Without circRNA PK datasets, rate constants are computed from literature ranges \citep{wesselhoeft2018,chen2019} via heuristic formulas mapping micro-predictions to biophysically plausible values, blended 70/30 with a learned Random Forest residual. The model outputs 72\,h trajectories for five modifications ($\Psi$, m6A, 5mC, I, unmodified) with endosomal escape of 1--5\% \citep{gilleron2013,hassett2019}.

\subsection{Sample-size-adaptive MOE ensemble}

Confluencia selects expert architectures by sample size \citep{jacobs1991}: Ridge + HGB for $N<80$; adding RF for $N<300$; adding MLP for $N\geq300$. Gating uses inverse-RMSE with biological constraints preventing expert collapse. Epitope sequences are encoded by Mamba3Lite \citep{gu2023}.

\subsection{ADMET evaluation}

Candidates are screened for PAINS patterns \citep{baell2010}, reactive groups \citep{brenk2008}, and rule-based ADMET flags from 2083-dimension features (Morgan fingerprints + physicochemical descriptors). Heuristic safety floors enforce physiological consistency: toxicity $\geq 0.06 + 0.16 \times \text{efficacy}$, inflammation $\geq 0.05 + 0.12 \times \text{efficacy} \times \text{binding}$.

\subsection{circRNA immunogenicity and microenvironment modeling}

ViennaRNA \citep{lorenz2011} predicts secondary structure; dsRNA $>$33\,bp flags PKR activation \citep{nallagatla2007}. Five innate immune pathways (RIG-I, TLR3, TLR7, PKR, OAS) are scored with modification-specific factors. TME characterization includes immune deconvolution (22 cell types), Cancer-Immunity Cycle scoring \citep{chen2018immunity}, and immune evasion assessment. Gene signatures (TROP2, Nectin-4, LIV1, B7H4, TMEM65) enable TNBC subtype evaluation.

\subsection{Uncertainty-adaptive five-dimension evaluation}

Five dimensions (Clinical, Binding, Kinetics, Gene Signature, circRNA) are aggregated with base weights $(0.30, 0.20, 0.15, 0.15, 0.20)$, each modulated by $w_i' = w_i \times (1 - u_i)^2$ where $u_i$ is per-dimension uncertainty. Under-constrained dimensions auto-downweight to near-zero. The composite yields Go ($\geq0.65$), Conditional ($\geq0.40$), or No-Go, with safety override at penalty $>0.30$.
