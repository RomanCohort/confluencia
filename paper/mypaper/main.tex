\begin{abstract}
\textbf{Summary:} Confluencia integrates circRNA pharmacokinetic modeling (half-life validated within 4.1\%), uncertainty-adaptive five-dimension evaluation, and cross-modality Go/Conditional/No-Go recommendations for circRNA therapeutic candidates. circRNA immunogenicity scores correlate with literature IFN data ($\rho{=}0.93$, $p{=}0.003$); 5D decisions remain stable under $\pm$50\% weight perturbation; gene signature target availability stratifies 3\,064 patients (log-rank $p{<}0.001$).\\
\textbf{Availability and implementation:} \url{https://github.com/RomanCohort/confluencia}, MIT license. Python 3.8+.\\
\textbf{Contact:} \email{18806370529@163.com}
\end{abstract}
