\subsection{RNACTM validation}

Half-life predictions match published values within 4.1\% \citep{wesselhoeft2018,chen2019}: unmodified (6.24 vs 6.0\,h), $\Psi$ (15.61 vs 15.0\,h), m6A (11.24 vs 10.8\,h). Endosomal escape (4.43\%) falls within 1--5\% \citep{gilleron2013}. Expression duration is overestimated (97 vs ${\sim}48$\,h).

\subsection{Small-sample ensemble}

On epitope efficacy ($N{=}300$, 5-fold CV), the adaptive MOE achieves 39.2\% MAE reduction over Ridge ($p<0.001$).

\subsection{Cross-module case study}
