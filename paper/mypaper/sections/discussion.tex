\section{Discussion}

Confluencia evaluates circRNA-specific dimensions absent from existing platforms (NetMHCpan, ADMETlab, Monolix) and closes the loop from evaluation to molecular optimization---no listed tool provides closed-loop candidate improvement. Under-constrained dimensions auto-downweight via $(1-u)^2$, reflecting data limitations.

Three lines of evidence support the platform: (1) immunogenicity scoring consistent with literature IFN data ($\rho{=}0.93$, $p{=}0.003$); (2) 5D decisions stable under $\pm$50\% weight perturbation; (3) gene signature target availability stratifies 3\,064 patients (log-rank $p{<}0.001$).

RNACTM pharmacokinetic predictions are consistent with published half-lives within 4.1\% for unmodified, m6A, and $\Psi$-modified circRNA \citep{wesselhoeft2018,chen2019}. The productive expression window (40\,h) is within 17\% of the published ${\sim}48$\,h range after incorporating late-phase accelerated clearance. Endosomal escape (5.2\%) is at the upper bound of the 1--5\% literature range \citep{gilleron2013}. MHC binding prediction covers 153 MHC-I alleles (HLA-A/B/C) and 37 MHC-II alleles (DRB1/DQ/DP), enabling CD4$^+$ T-cell epitope screening alongside CD8$^+$.

The absence of circRNA-specific pharmacokinetic datasets motivated our uncertainty-adaptive design: RNACTM rate constants are derived from literature priors, and under-constrained dimensions auto-downweight via $(1-u)^2$. Remaining limitations: (1) immunogenicity consistency checking uses the same literature that informed scoring rules; (2) gene signature stratifies patients by target availability (high vs low tertile: 57\% vs 20\% death rate), but C-index is modest (0.52), reflecting that the score captures therapeutic target availability rather than intrinsic disease risk; (3) circRNA ADMET maps classical endpoints to circRNA analogs rather than direct experimental toxicology data; (4) only half-life and immunogenicity among five circRNA functional dimensions have been benchmarked---translation potential, miRNA sponge, and RBP binding lack independent evaluation. Wet-lab validation in our lab is underway to provide the first independent circRNA PK data.
