\subsection{RNACTM validation}

Half-life predictions match published values within 4.1\% for three modifications with available data: unmodified (6.24\,h vs 6.0\,h), $\Psi$ (15.61\,h vs 15.0\,h), and m6A (11.24\,h vs 10.8\,h) \citep{wesselhoeft2018,chen2019}. 5mC and I modifications lack published circRNA half-life data for validation. Endosomal escape fraction (4.43\%) falls within the 1--5\% range \citep{gilleron2013}. Protein expression duration is overestimated (97\,h vs ${\sim}48$\,h), indicating clearance needs refinement.

\subsection{Small-sample ensemble}

On epitope efficacy ($N{=}300$, 5-fold CV), the sample-size-adaptive MOE achieves 39.2\% MAE reduction over Ridge ($p<0.001$), confirming that adaptive gating benefits the small-sample regime characteristic of circRNA wet-lab studies.

\subsection{Cross-module case study}

Three circRNA--drug pairs were evaluated through the full pipeline (Table~\ref{tab:casestudy}):
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{$\Psi$-circRNA + Aspirin}: Extended half-life (${\sim}15$\,h) with low ADMET risk yields Go/Conditional---consistent with $\Psi$'s known stability advantage.
\item \textbf{Unmodified circRNA + Rhodanine}: Short half-life (6.24\,h), high immune activation, PAINS scaffold → No-Go with safety override---consistent with Rhodanine's known interference.
\item \textbf{5mC-circRNA + Ibuprofen}: Intermediate half-life (${\sim}8$\,h), moderate ADMET → Conditional reflecting the 5D trade-off.
\end{itemize}

These recommendations emerge from cross-module reasoning that no single specialized tool can produce, as existing tools lack circRNA-specific dimensions entirely.
