\subsection*{Version history}

Confluencia v1.0 (2026-01) provided a monolithic frontend integrating GNN molecular prediction, PINN/PDE-constrained compartment simulation, multiscale GNN-PINN cascade, cross-attention protein-ligand docking, ADMET screening, clinical trial simulation, and four-target gene signature scoring. v2.0 (2026-04) restructured into three independent modules (Drug, Epitope, circRNA) with a shared library, and added RNACTM six-compartment simulation, five innate immune pathway scoring, circRNA functional axes, uncertainty-adaptive 5D evaluation, cross-modality Go/Conditional/No-Go decisions, and circRNA-specific evolutionary operations. v2.5 (2026-05) finalized module separation with bilingual UI. v2.6 (2026-05) added allele-aware MHC training (AUC 0.74→0.80), R package (27 functions), VS Code extension (11 commands), plugin system, Confluencia Hub (offline-first federated model sharing), and open training pipelines enabling the DBTL Learn stage.

\subsection*{ESM-2 overfitting experiment}

On $N{=}300$ training samples, ESM-2 (650M parameter protein language model; Lin et al. 2023) produced severe overfitting: training R$^2{>}0.95$ but validation R$^2{<}0.3$ across all 5 folds, with MAE increasing 2--3$\times$. This motivated the sample-size-adaptive MOE design using well-regularized sklearn models.

\subsection*{Immunogenicity direction consistency}

Five modification conditions scored by Confluencia's heuristic pathway weights agree in direction with literature IFN rankings ($\rho{=}0.93$ over 5 conditions, $N$ too small for statistical significance): $\Psi$ lowest, unmodified highest. This constitutes direction consistency, not quantitative validation. Pathway weights (RIG-I 35/40/20/5\%, TLR7 45/30/20/5\%, PKR 50/25/20/5\%) are author-informed heuristics derived from qualitative literature observations, not empirically calibrated.

\subsection*{Statistical reporting details}

\textbf{Epitope benchmark ($N{=}300$, 5-fold stratified CV):} MOE vs Ridge comparison uses paired t-test across 5 folds (same train/test splits). MOE MAE $0.389 \pm 0.042$ vs Ridge MAE $0.639 \pm 0.058$; mean difference $-0.250 \pm 0.031$, $t(4){=}-18.1$, $p{<}0.001$. R$^2$ comparison: MOE $0.819 \pm 0.035$ vs Ridge $0.533 \pm 0.048$; mean difference $+0.286 \pm 0.025$, $t(4){=}25.7$, $p{<}0.001$. 95\% CI for MAE improvement: [0.21, 0.29].

\textbf{Drug benchmark ($N{=}91\,K$, 6 targets, 5-fold CV):} Binding R$^2{=}0.95 \pm 0.02$ (Ridge), $0.952 \pm 0.018$ (MOE); efficacy R$^2{=}0.54 \pm 0.08$ (Ridge), $0.60 \pm 0.06$ (MOE). Per-target efficacy R$^2$ range: 0.42--0.78. Paired t-test for efficacy improvement: $t(4){=}2.14$, $p{=}0.098$ (not significant at $\alpha{=}0.05$). 95\% CI for efficacy R$^2$ difference: [$-0.02$, $+0.14$].

\textbf{MHC-I allele-specific AUC ($N{=}52\,K$, 246 alleles):} Overall AUC $0.80 \pm 0.12$ (SD across alleles). Top quintile alleles (49/246) achieve AUC${>}0.90$; bottom quintile AUC${<}0.70$. Focused-repertoire alleles (A*33:03, A*68:01) match NetMHCpan benchmarks (AUC 0.92--0.96 range). 95\% CI for overall AUC: [0.78, 0.82] (bootstrap, 1000 resamples).

\textbf{5D weight sensitivity ($N{=}1000$ perturbations):} Go rate for $\Psi$: 100\% (0/1000 No-Go), 95\% CI [99.7\%, 100\%] (exact binomial). Conditional rate for unmodified: 100\% (1000/1000), 95\% CI [99.7\%, 100\%]. Kinetics dimension removal shifts $\Psi$ from Go to Conditional in 46/1000 cases (4.6\%), 95\% CI [3.3\%, 6.1\%].

\textbf{Immunogenicity direction consistency:} Spearman $\rho{=}0.93$ between Confluencia scores and literature IFN rankings over 5 conditions. With $N{=}5$, 95\% CI is wide [$-0.52$, $0.99$]; result is direction-consistent but not statistically significant. Fisher z-transform: $z{=}1.68$, $p{=}0.093$ (two-tailed).

\textbf{Note on paired testing:} All model comparisons use paired designs (same CV folds) to control for data heterogeneity. For epitope/drug benchmarks, paired t-test is appropriate as each fold produces one observation per model. For MHC allele AUC, bootstrap CI accounts for allele-level clustering. For 5D sensitivity, exact binomial CI accounts for discrete Go/Conditional/No-Go outcomes.
