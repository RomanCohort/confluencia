\section{Methods}

\subsection{RNACTM pharmacokinetic model}

RNACTM simulates circRNA concentration through six compartments via $\dot{\mathbf{x}} = A\mathbf{x}$ with 14 rate constants \citep{liu2023}. Without circRNA PK datasets, rate constants are computed from literature ranges \citep{wesselhoeft2018,chen2019} via heuristic formulas, blended 70/30 with a learned Random Forest residual. Outputs: 72\,h trajectories for five modifications ($\Psi$, m6A, 5mC, I, unmodified) with endosomal escape 1--5\% \citep{gilleron2013,hassett2019}.

\subsection{Small-molecule evaluation}

Expert architectures are selected by sample size \citep{jacobs1991}: Ridge + HGB ($N<80$); adding RF ($N<300$); adding MLP ($N\geq300$), with inverse-RMSE gating and biological constraints preventing collapse. Epitope sequences use Mamba3Lite encoding \citep{gu2023}. ADMET screening covers PAINS \citep{baell2010}, reactive groups \citep{brenk2008}, and 2083-dimension feature flags, with heuristic safety floors (toxicity $\geq 0.06 + 0.16 \times \text{efficacy}$; inflammation $\geq 0.05 + 0.12 \times \text{efficacy} \times \text{binding}$).

\subsection{circRNA functional assessment}

The circRNA module covers structure, immunogenicity, and functional interactions (Table~\ref{tab:coverage}). ViennaRNA \citep{lorenz2011} predicts secondary structure; dsRNA $>$33\,bp flags PKR activation \citep{nallagatla2007}. Five innate immune pathways (RIG-I, TLR3, TLR7, PKR, OAS) receive modification-specific scoring. Functional axes include miRNA sponge potential, RBP interaction networks, translation potential (ORF/IRES/NMD), RNA modification site prediction (m6A/$\Psi$/m5C/m1A), and RNA--ligand docking for direct drug--circRNA binding. TME characterization covers immune deconvolution (22 cell types), Cancer-Immunity Cycle \citep{chen2018immunity}, immune evasion, and TNBC subtype signatures. Drug sensitivity is predicted from circRNA expression; survival analysis (KM/Cox) links expression to clinical outcomes.

\subsection{Uncertainty-adaptive five-dimension evaluation}

Five dimensions (Clinical, Binding, Kinetics, Gene Signature, circRNA) are aggregated with base weights $(0.30, 0.20, 0.15, 0.15, 0.20)$, each modulated by $w_i' = w_i \times (1 - u_i)^2$ where $u_i$ is per-dimension uncertainty. Under-constrained dimensions auto-downweight. The composite yields Go ($\geq0.65$), Conditional ($\geq0.40$), or No-Go, with safety override at penalty $>0.30$.
