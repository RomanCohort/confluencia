\subsection{RNACTM validation}

Half-life predictions match published values within 4.1\%: unmodified (6.24 vs 6.0\,h), $\Psi$ (15.61 vs 15.0\,h), m6A (11.24 vs 10.8\,h) \citep{wesselhoeft2018,chen2019}. 5mC and I lack published data. Endosomal escape (4.43\%) falls within 1--5\% \citep{gilleron2013}. Protein expression duration is overestimated (97 vs ${\sim}48$\,h).

\subsection{Small-sample ensemble}

On epitope efficacy ($N{=}300$, 5-fold CV), the adaptive MOE achieves 39.2\% MAE reduction over Ridge ($p<0.001$).

\subsection{Cross-module case study}

Three circRNA--drug pairs illustrate cross-module reasoning (Table~\ref{tab:casestudy}): $\Psi$-circRNA + Aspirin yields Go/Conditional; unmodified circRNA + Rhodanine yields No-Go (PAINS + safety override); 5mC-circRNA + Ibuprofen yields Conditional. These emerge from cross-module reasoning no single tool can produce.
