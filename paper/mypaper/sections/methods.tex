\section{Methods}

\subsection{RNACTM compartment simulation}

RNACTM simulates circRNA through six compartments (Injection $\rightarrow$ LNP $\rightarrow$ Endosome $\rightarrow$ Cytoplasmic RNA $\rightarrow$ Protein $\rightarrow$ Clearance) via $\dot{\mathbf{x}} = A\mathbf{x}$ with literature-derived rate constants \citep{wesselhoeft2018,chen2019,gilleron2013,paunovska2018} and late-phase accelerated clearance after ${\sim}48$\,h. This is a deterministic simulation, not a population PK model fitted to concentration--time data; it provides compartment dynamics without inter-individual variability. $\pm$20\% rate constant variation changes half-life by $\leq$8.2\%.

\subsection{Small-molecule evaluation}

Expert architectures are selected by sample size \citep{jacobs1991}: Ridge+HGB ($N{<}80$); adding RF ($N{<}300$); adding MLP ($N{\geq}300$), with inverse-RMSE gating. ESM-2 \citep{lin2023} overfits at $N{<}300$; sklearn models provide stable predictions without GPU. Epitope sequences use Mamba3Lite encoding \citep{gu2023}; MHC features cover 153 class-I and 37 class-II alleles with allele-specific pseudo-sequence encoding; 56 additional alleles use allele-agnostic features (total 246). ADMET screening covers PAINS \citep{baell2010} and safety floors.

\subsection{circRNA functional assessment}

ViennaRNA \citep{lorenz2011} predicts secondary structure; five innate immune pathways (RIG-I, TLR3, TLR7, PKR, OAS) receive modification-specific heuristic scoring (author-informed weights, not empirically calibrated). Per-dimension uncertainty: half-life and immunogenicity $u{=}0.15$ (direction-consistency verified); miRNA sponge, RBP binding, translation potential $u{=}0.8$ (no independent validation), yielding effective weights $(1{-}0.8)^2{=}0.04$ of nominal.

\subsection{Uncertainty-adaptive 5D evaluation}

Five dimensions (Clinical 0.30, Binding 0.20, Kinetics 0.15, Gene Signature 0.15, circRNA 0.20) are aggregated with $w_i' = w_i \times (1 - u_i)^2$. Gene Signature (C-index 0.52) is flagged as exploratory. Composite yields Go ($\geq0.65$), Conditional ($\geq0.40$), or No-Go, with safety override at penalty $>0.30$.
