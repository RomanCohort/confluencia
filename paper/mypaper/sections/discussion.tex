\section{Discussion}

Confluencia's contribution is cross-modality reasoning, not outperforming siloed tools (NetMHCpan: AUC $>0.92$ \citep{reynisson2020}; ADMETlab: $>50$ endpoints \citep{xiong2024}). The case study illustrates: $\Psi$-circRNA + Rhodanine receives No-Go because circRNA immunogenicity compounds Rhodanine's PAINS profile---a conclusion requiring both modalities. NetMHCpan reports binding; ADMETlab flags PAINS; neither connects these to circRNA half-life or immune activation. This cross-modality integration is the architectural innovation.

The uncertainty-adaptive 5D evaluation reinforces this: under-constrained dimensions auto-downweight via $(1-u)^2$, communicating limitations rather than hiding them.

Limitations: (1) RNACTM overestimates expression duration (97 vs 48\,h); (2) ADMET QSAR lacks circRNA toxicity data; (3) only MHC-I alleles. As circRNA PK datasets emerge, ODE parameters can transition from heuristic to data-fitted without architectural redesign.
